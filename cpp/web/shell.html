How to play

Tap the map to walk, or use the directional pad. Hold an arrow to keep moving; tap a conversation to continue, or drag its text to scroll. Change touch controls in Settings → Controls. The pad stays on the right.

Full screen requests portrait orientation on phones where supported. You can also turn your phone sideways for a larger game outside full screen.

Keyboard controls

Arrows / WASD to move. Enter / Space to continue dialogue. I for inventory. Esc for the game menu. Q to quick save; Shift+Q to quick restore. K/L for save/load slots; Shift+L to restore the latest floor autosave. O for settings; H for help.

Your saves

Use the in-game Game menu to save and load. Saves stay in this browser. Export a copy to keep them safe before clearing site data or switching browsers.
