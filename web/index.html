WELCOME TO THE TOWER

A little strategy goes a long way.

You are a hero trapped in the Sorcerer’s tower. Find keys, collect treasures, and climb through its floors. Your strength grows with every careful decision.

⌨

Find your way

Use arrow keys or WASD to move one square. On touch screens, use the directional buttons or swipe the board.

⚔

Pick your battles

Move toward a monster to fight. Hover or tap a monster to inspect the damage first. Each battle is resolved by the Rust game engine.

⚿

Spend keys carefully

Step into a door to use a key of the matching color. Health potions and gems are collected when you walk into them.

◇

Keep your progress

Your adventure resumes automatically in this browser. Press Save game to keep a checkpoint you can restore from the menu. You can also export saves, import them, or load a custom campaign.
