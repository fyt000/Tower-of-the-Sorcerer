# Early-area considerations

Use these considerations when planning the first-cycle opening and floor-10 arena. Determine access, purchase timing and resource reserves from the current board and forecasts. A completed opening does not establish an ideal route or purchase sequence.

## Plan the opening from current mechanics

First-cycle capture resets HP to 400 and attack/defense to 10 and removes equipment in that branch. The opening's high starting stats do not justify clearing hypothetical reachable rooms before capture. Verify actual access and preserved keys/items in the live state.

The early sword and shield are useful equipment milestones because their gains can make later return visits cheaper. Compare their access costs with any intervening gems or necessary fights; their order and purchase timing still depend on the current route. Source or clues can identify concealed entrances, which require deliberate native contact rather than ordinary road navigation.

Reserve keys for progression and inspect the actual floor-6/floor-7 merchant offers when comparing access options. There is no general prohibition on buying keys. A stat purchase can open a breakpoint; an HP purchase should be considered only after checking cheaper routes and available supplies.

Flight requires adjacent-stair entry and uses native preview direction, ceiling and landing rules. Check the resulting square before continuing. Price a walking route by its actual doors, fights and supplies; do not assume a particular Flight landing bypasses them.

Before triggering the floor-10 arena, budget all required guards and the boss as one sequence. Inspect how the scene rearranges enemies and closes access; refresh the board before choosing destinations. Individual survivability markers do not establish enough HP for the full arena. Recalculate with current stats, forecasts and supplies.

After equipment upgrades, reconsider deferred rooms before advancing through expensive blockers. Optional enemies may still be worth leaving for Lucky Gold. Collect the boss's supplies and inspect the newly opened route before choosing the next milestone.
